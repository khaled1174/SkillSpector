# Patterns

No patterns are promoted yet.

A pattern may be promoted only after multiple supporting learnings point to the same durable rule. Do not author broad doctrine from a single incident.

## Promotion gate

- At least two related learnings support the rule.
- Current source files and oracle output do not contradict it.
- The rule improves future planning/review/debugging.
- High-risk changes require Khaled approval.
